# Finrep

> AI-powered financial reporting intelligence platform for CFO offices. Finrep helps SEC Reporting, Technical Accounting, Legal Counsel, Investor Relations, Disclosure Committee, Internal Audit, CFO/Controller, Tax, and ESG Reporting teams research, compare, validate, and draft disclosures using SEC filings, EDGAR data, and IR materials — with every answer linked to inspectable source documents.

## Core Capabilities

- Comment Letter Explorer: Search and analyze SEC comment letters across public filings.
- SEC Filing Analysis: AI-powered analysis of 10-K, 10-Q, and other EDGAR filings.
- Technical Accounting Research: Automated research across ASC, IFRS, and regulatory guidance.
- Financial Disclosure Intelligence: Benchmarking and peer comparison for financial disclosures.
- Section 16 Compliance: Prepare and file Forms 3, 4, and 5 directly to EDGAR.
